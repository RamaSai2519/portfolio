01 — the rule, one closed candle at a time

Move, build-up, breakout

The setup detector is fed one Nifty 1-minute candle after it closes and nothing else, so it cannot see the future: no look-ahead is a fact of its signature, not a promise.

nifty · 1 min09:15 → decision before 12:00
P* floor
move · ≥3 closes, ≥0.10% of spot build-up · ≥5 candles, width ≤60% of move breakout close → CE, below → PE floor touch → exit in candle

  1. Pick the option

    Nearest expiry, highest open interest within ATM ± 2 strikes.

  2. Check the depth

    Buy share of the book must be at least 0.90, from a row no older than 90 s.

  3. Size and buy

    Half the capital, limit order at the live ask.

  4. Lock the floor

    P* = entry + (Rs 2,000 + costs) / qty. A touch exits in that candle.

  5. Or get out

    Back inside the range, a Rs 10,000 hard stop, or 15:15. One trade a day.

Every 30 s the broker's orders, fills and positions are compared with Emporos's own. Any difference stops new entries. An ambiguous order is UNKNOWN until the broker is asked by its tag.
03 — risk engine

Sixteen rules, checked in order, every order

A pure, ordered list over an immutable snapshot of the account, so every rule is unit-testable. A rejection is always stored with its full context: a signal that silently vanished could not be audited.
