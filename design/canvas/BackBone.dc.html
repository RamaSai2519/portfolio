02 — the scheduler

Every five minutes, four lanes at once

A CloudWatch rule fires the scheduler Lambda. A thread pool runs four job groups side by side, and due calls and messages go back through the API's own /actions/call and /actions/send_whatsapp, so there is one path for everything.

rate(5 minutes)

events_reminders_jobslist upcoming → remind
experts_jobsauto online → status check
misc_jobsread lead emails → back up db
03 — one call, across the platform

Booked here, placed here, scored by mark8

  1. A senior books a slot

    In the app, or through ARK-I on WhatsApp.

  2. The schedule is stored

    Recurring bookings expand into one-off schedules.

  3. A scheduler tick finds it due

    Nudges both people on WhatsApp and pings Slack.

  4. The call is placed

    Through /actions/call; the call platform reports back over webhooks.

  5. mark8 reads and scores it

    Topics, personas and a conversation score land on the call.

  6. Problems escalate

    Escalations reach the team before anyone has to ask.
